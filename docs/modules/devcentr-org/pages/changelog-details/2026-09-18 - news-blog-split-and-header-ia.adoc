= 2026-09-18 — News/Blog split and header IA
:navtitle: 2026-09-18 News, Blog, and nav

== Summary

Marketing site channels and primary navigation were realigned so outward news and inward blog are separate surfaces, with catalogue routes grouped under Assets.

== News and Blog

* `/blog` is a first-class channel (`content/blog/`, `blog-posts.generated.json`, `/blog` feeds) — not an alias of `/news`.
* News remains outward shared record in `content/news/`.
* Editorial voice rules live in `content/news/README.adoc`.

== Header navigation

* **Assets** dropdown — `/assets` gate, `/apps`, `/skills`, `/assets/standards` (Apps vs Standards split).
* **Updates** dropdown — `/news`, `/blog`, `/changelog`, external docs hub.
* **Help** replaces the former Community entry; **Links** holds outbound shortcuts.

== Routes and CI

* `/assets` and `/assets/standards` added to `scripts/site-routes.mjs` for prerender, SPA fallback, and sitemap (2026-09-19 follow-up).
