== Hero orbit sits right of center

The homepage hero orbit was offset from the right edge of the section, so on a wide window it stayed glued to the screen. Its center now sits 300px to the right of the hero midline (`left: calc(50% + 300px)` and a half-width shift). Vertical placement is unchanged, and a narrow viewport still clips it with the hero's overflow.
