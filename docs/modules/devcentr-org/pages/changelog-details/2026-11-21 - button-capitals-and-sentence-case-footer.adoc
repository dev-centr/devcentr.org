== Button capitals and a sentence-case footer

Primary, outline, and secondary buttons take capitals from `--text-case`. The label text stays in sentence case.

The footer leaves that pattern. Column labels and links are sentence case. Tracking, the space between letters, is `0.04em` instead of the wide `0.22em` used for capitals. Category labels stay lighter than the links, not heavier.
