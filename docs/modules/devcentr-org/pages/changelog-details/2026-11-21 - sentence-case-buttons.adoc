== Sentence-case buttons

Button labels no longer use a monospace face or uppercase tracking. They use Montserrat at the button's normal size and weight, with the wording as written: "Get App", "Stack Advisor", and the rest.

Nav items and the other small meta labels stay IBM Plex Mono in capitals.
