== What belongs here

* Initiatives and direction changes across the Dev-Centr org
* Notable shipping moments on the landing site, docs, and related tools

This is **not** a replacement for Antora changelogs or issue trackers. Those stay the system of record for specs and day-to-day work. The blog is the narrative layer—what we chose, why it matters, and where to dig deeper.
