= We're opening a News desk
:description: Why DevCentr is starting a public News feed—and what you won't find here.
:revdate: 2026-07-24
:keywords: news, blog, chronicle, initiatives, DevCentr

Dev-Centr has been shipping in public for a while—docs, tools, the app, the whole messy orbit of related projects. What we haven't had is a place to *talk about* that work like people talk about it: the why, the bets, the “here's where we're headed” pieces.

So we're starting News at link:https://devcentr.org/news[devcentr.org/news].

Think magazine column more than release notes. If something changes how we build, how we show up as an org, or what we're trying to become, it belongs here. If it's “we fixed a heading and renamed three pages,” that's the link:https://devcentr.org/changelog[Changelog].

We'll write when there's something worth saying. No weekly quota. No content mill. Subscribe to the link:https://devcentr.org/news/rss.xml[RSS] or link:https://devcentr.org/news/atom.xml[Atom] feed if you want a ping when we do.

Elsewhere, as always: link:https://docs.devcentr.org[the docs], link:https://github.com/dev-centr[the repos], and link:https://devcentr.app[the app].
