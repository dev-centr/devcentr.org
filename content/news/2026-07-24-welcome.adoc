We are starting a public **News** feed on link:https://devcentr.org/news[devcentr.org/news] (legacy path: link:https://devcentr.org/blog[/blog]).

== What belongs here

* Initiatives and direction changes across the Dev-Centr org
* Notable shipping moments on the landing site, docs, and related tools
* Cross-project news that affects how we build (tooling, formats, sister orgs)
* Highlights mirrored from Antora changelogs (docs remain the system of record)

This is **not** a replacement for Antora changelogs or issue trackers. Those stay the system of record for specs and day-to-day work. News is the narrative layer—what we chose, why it matters, and where to dig deeper.
