= Brand mark stays in open SVG
:description: The DevCentr mark ships as open SVG motion — not a proprietary player dependency for an open brand.
:revdate: 2026-07-24
:keywords: news, brand, SVG, design

The DevCentr brand mark stays in open SVG, including motion that does not require a proprietary player.

That choice keeps an open-source org's identity readable in ordinary browsers and docs — without baking a closed runtime into the logo.
