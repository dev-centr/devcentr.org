= Stop dumping people into the docs
:description: We built a Help hub so “I need assistance” doesn't mean “go read the manual.”
:revdate: 2026-07-24
:keywords: news, blog, help, support, status, community, DevCentr

Here's a quiet failure mode a lot of open projects share: someone shows up stuck, and the only door we offer is *documentation*. Docs are great when you want to learn. They're a cold shoulder when you want a person—or at least a clear path to one.

So we put up a Help hub at link:https://devcentr.org/help[devcentr.org/help].

The idea is simple. **Help** is the umbrella. Under it you can choose *self-help* (docs, guides, the knowledge base) or *support* (talk to us). Status and community channels sit next to both, so you're not hunting through the footer for Slack or “is the site up?”

We don't have a polished ticket desk yet—and we're not going to pretend we do. For now, mail link:mailto:support@devcentr.org[support@devcentr.org]. A real assisted desk (tickets, maybe AI triage) is on the roadmap; we'd rather ship an honest front door than a half-built system with a shiny button.

If you just want the pulse of our public surfaces, link:https://devcentr.org/status[/status] runs live probes. Longer uptime history still lives at link:https://status.devcentr.org[status.devcentr.org].

This is a small change on the site. It's a bigger change in posture: we assume visitors arrive with different jobs to do, and we stop treating “read the docs” as the only answer.
