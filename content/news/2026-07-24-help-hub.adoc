We added a public **Help** surface at link:https://devcentr.org/help[devcentr.org/help] so visitors are not dumped straight into docs when they want assistance.

== What shipped

* **`/help`** — choose *self-help* (documentation) or *support* (assisted help; desk coming soon)
* **`/support`** — alias that lands on the support section of Help
* **`/status`** — client-side probes of public endpoints (`/health` still redirects here)
* Header **Community** menu and footer Community column (Help, Status, Slack, Discussions, uptime monitor)
* Interim contact: `support@devcentr.org`

A dedicated ticket / AI-assisted desk is planned; we are not linking a half-built system yet. Planning notes live in the site repo under `docs/help-and-support-plans.adoc`.

== How to think about it

*Help* is the top-level concept. Documentation is self-help; contacting us is support. Status and community channels sit alongside both.

Hosted uptime history (Better Stack) remains at link:https://status.devcentr.org[status.devcentr.org], linked from the probe page and Help.
