= New additions to the Business Bootstrap guide
:description: Scheduling, design, and vault capability areas land in Business Bootstrap on docs.devcentr.org — growth in the same support-systems guide.
:revdate: 2026-08-08
:keywords: news, blog, Business Bootstrap, Cal.com, Penpot, Bitwarden, Vaultwarden, docs

**Business Bootstrap** on link:https://docs.devcentr.org/business-bootstrap/latest/index.html[docs.devcentr.org] already covered the email matrix and org `infra` pattern. It still does — and now it also has room for the neighboring support systems every tech company ends up standing up anyway.

What landed in this pass:

* **Scheduling** — Cal.com (hosted) vs Cal.diy (MIT self-host)
* **Design** — Penpot as the open canvas default; Figma when partners already live there
* **Vault** — Bitwarden cloud vs official self-host vs Vaultwarden

Stage defaults still stitch the areas together. Product partner marketplaces stay elsewhere; this guide is for the IT skeleton.

Visit the link:https://docs.devcentr.org/business-bootstrap/latest/index.html[Business Bootstrap guide].

For the suggested defaults across email, scheduling, design, vault, chat, org infra, and funding, visit the link:https://docs.devcentr.org/business-bootstrap/software-stack/[Software stack].
