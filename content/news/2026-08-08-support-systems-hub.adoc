= Business Bootstrap becomes a support-systems hub
:description: Founder ops docs grow past email—scheduling, design, and vaults get their own capability areas on docs.devcentr.org.
:revdate: 2026-08-08
:keywords: news, blog, Business Bootstrap, Cal.com, Penpot, Bitwarden, Vaultwarden, docs

**Business Bootstrap** on link:https://docs.devcentr.org/business-bootstrap/[docs.devcentr.org] is no longer just the email matrix and org `infra` pattern. It is a **support-systems hub**: Email, Scheduling, Design, Vault, and Org infra—each with room for explanation, how-tos, and interconnect notes.

What landed first under the new shape:

* **Scheduling** — Cal.com (hosted) vs Cal.diy (MIT self-host)
* **Design** — Penpot as the open canvas default; Figma when partners already live there
* **Vault** — Bitwarden cloud vs official self-host vs Vaultwarden

The stack map and stage defaults stitch the areas together. Product partner marketplaces stay elsewhere; this hub is for the IT skeleton every tech company ends up building anyway.

Start at the link:https://docs.devcentr.org/business-bootstrap/[hub index] or the link:https://docs.devcentr.org/business-bootstrap/reference/business-resources.html[stack map].
