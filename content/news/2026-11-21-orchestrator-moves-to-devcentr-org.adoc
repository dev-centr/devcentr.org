= Orchestrator moves to devcentr.org
:description: The flagship Development Orchestration Suite is now named Orchestrator and lives at devcentr.org/orchestrator, with devcentr.app forwarding there and rewritten copy across devcentr.org.
:revdate: 2026-11-21
:keywords: news, Orchestrator, devcentr.org, devcentr.app, release, site update

The flagship Development Orchestration Suite is now named **Orchestrator** and lives at link:https://devcentr.org/orchestrator[devcentr.org/orchestrator].
`devcentr.app` now forwards directly to `/orchestrator`, retiring the split between two separate sites and bringing downloads, documentation links, and the rest of the DevCentr ecosystem under one roof.

The rename settles a distinction that had been blurry when the desktop application and the organization shared the same word.
Under the new framing, the developer is the conductor and **Orchestrator** is the instrument.
The desktop suite for Windows, macOS, and Linux handles ecosystem management, visual architecture blueprints, shared AI context, reproducible workspaces, and DevOps as one integrated lifecycle around the code.

== One home, two clear surfaces

Previously, visitors bouncing between `devcentr.org` and `devcentr.app` crossed into two different color palettes, type treatments, and overlapping summaries.
Moving the product sheet onto `devcentr.org/orchestrator` unifies the experience under the DevCentr teal identity and Space Grotesk typography, while giving each surface a single job.

The root site at link:https://devcentr.org[devcentr.org] serves as the home base for the wider ecosystem: the orbital system map, toolchain architecture overview, Assets and Apps catalogs, Stack Advisor, Agent skills, Extensions store, writing, and support.
Clicking **Download** on the homepage leads straight to link:/orchestrator[/orchestrator], which focuses strictly on the desktop suite's installers, portable builds, and five core capabilities without repeating the organization's overview.

== Rewritten copy across devcentr.org

Alongside the consolidation, public copy across `devcentr.org` received a full editorial pass.
Every major route, including the homepage, `/orchestrator`, the Assets and Apps catalogs, Stack Advisor, Agent skills, the Extensions store, interactive demos, Help, Status, and Changelog, was rewritten for direct, plain-language clarity.
Catalog entries and project overviews were updated from shorthand repository notes into readable summaries, stale references to "DevCentr desktop" were updated to Orchestrator, and `/status` added a live probe for `/blog/`.

The editorial audit also surfaced recurring patterns in how agent-assisted drafts leak internal notes, defensive phrasing, and repeated disclaimers into public pages.
A companion essay on the blog, link:/blog/2026-11-21-when-your-website-talks-like-an-agent[When your website talks like an agent], walks through the concrete before-and-after examples from the rewrite.

Downloads, capability details, and documentation links for the suite are live now at link:/orchestrator[devcentr.org/orchestrator], with guides and architecture references continuing at link:https://docs.devcentr.org[docs.devcentr.org].
